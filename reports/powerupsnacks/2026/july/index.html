02 · Influencer intelligence

Twelve creators are doing the work a paid budget usually pays for.

Across the brand window (21 July 2025 to 5 July 2026), Power Up earned twelve Creator Tagged Posts (Instagram and TikTok posts where a creator tagged the brand inside the window) from twelve different creators. The obvious read is that this is a small number; the more useful read is where those posts came from and how far one of them travelled.

On scale, creator content is not a fringe format here: it is more than a third of everything live, and it is the format Power Up's own earned creators already produce for free. The four brands leaning hardest into it, Gimme Seaweed (80% creator-shot), The Nut Dreamers (75%), Boundless (70%) and No Cow (60%), are building their paid libraries almost entirely out of creator videos.

On what survives, creator-shot UGC is the durable format. Six brands, among them No Cow, GoMacro and The Nut Dreamers, sustain creator videos built on a fast high-protein recipe hook, each still live past 30 days and the longest running 274 days. Two more, Gimme Seaweed and Boundless, keep a picky-kid lunchbox parenting hook alive past 250 days. These are exactly the hosting and better-for-you angles the creators tagging Power Up already film.

On who commissions at volume, the testing cadence is high. Clif keeps 136 creatives live and cycles most within a week, Nature's Garden holds 121 with a 29-day median runtime, and Oats Overnight runs 78. These brands are not betting on one hero video; they are producing enough creator variants to test constantly. Power Up, sitting on twelve earned creators and zero paid creatives, has the supply side and none of the distribution side.

05 · Feed content strategy

Your seasonal posts win, yet more than half the feed is product flatlays.

Power Up's own feed is the quietest signal in this report, and the most fixable. Across the brand window (21 July 2025 to 5 July 2026) the account published nine Instagram posts, six carousels and three videos, averaging 13 likes and a median of 11 against 42,186 followers. There is a more useful signal underneath those averages: not all of the nine posts perform the same, and the ones that do best are the ones the brand makes least.

Format tells the same story. Carousels take four of the top five slots and average 14 likes across the six in the window, edging the three videos at 11. The best post, the autumn three-variety carousel, is a low-production styled product shot rather than a polished studio set, which fits the category pattern where lighter creator-style production tends to out-perform brand-polished frames.

The multiplier is small in absolute terms but clear in shape: the top Seasonal/Timely post at 23 likes runs roughly double the 11-like average of the Relatable/Meme flatlays that fill the feed. Publishing more seasonal product storytelling and less studio flatlay is the single change the numbers point the content team toward.

The Biggest Miss

Your Seasonal/Timely posts average 1.4× the likes of your Relatable/Meme posts, yet more than half your own feed is Relatable/Meme product flatlays. The own feed averages 13 likes against 42,186 followers, so the creators tagging you are out-reaching your own channel by three to four orders of magnitude.

07 · The opportunity

Six moves that change the game for Power Up.

Every move here scales a signal Power Up already produces rather than manufacturing a new one. The order follows the arc of the report: make the earned engine always-on, put it where the category spends, and turn retail distribution into tracked sales.

OPPORTUNITY 01
Turn the 4th-of-July burst into an always-on Creator Tagged Posts retainer bench

Power Up already earns twelve Creator Tagged Posts from twelve creators, led by a 597,749-view 4th-of-July Reel from @lifewithciji, rank 49 of 155 in this dataset on Total Creator Views.

Ten of those twelve posts landed in a single two-week window through one BabbleBoxx box, and the rest of the year is quiet. A small bench of five to eight family and better-for-you creators posting monthly would compound a signal Power Up already produces.

OPPORTUNITY 02
Revive the dormant TikTok with commissioned Creator Tagged Posts

@powerupsnacks holds a TikTok handle but posted nothing to its own feed in the window, and the only Power Up TikTok reach came from a scatter of small #ad creators.

The category's biggest trail-mix and snack views are on TikTok, where recipe and snack-hack Reels routinely clear hundreds of thousands of views from mid-tier creators. A commissioned TikTok creator squad opens a platform the brand has left on the table.

Competitors running it: good culture, Feastables, Legendary Foods.

Put creator content into paid to match category testing cadence

About 35% of the category's longest-running winning ads are creator-shot UGC, the format Power Up's earned creators already produce for free. Nature's Garden alone sustains 121 live creatives; licensing a handful of creator videos into paid would let Power Up test at the same cadence.
