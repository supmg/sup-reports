Rizz Superfood Gummies — Campaign Strategy Recommendations

Four creator plays Rizz should run, each proven by the most similar winning brands: UGC for Brand Feed, Long-Term Ambassador, Creator Collective, and Always-On Retainers.

Strategy 01

UGC for Brand Feed

Pay creators to shoot video, then run that footage as posts on your own grid — instead of filling the feed in-house.

Why this, for Rizz

Rizz fills its own grid in-house and tops out around three likes a post. Olly and Lemme pay creators to shoot the video and run it as their own feed: Lemme's four creators below alone pulled 5.7M views and 497,871 total engagements. The output instinct is right — the creator input is missing.

Strategy 02

Long-Term Brand Ambassador

Keep one or a few creators on a standing relationship so they post about you repeatedly over months, not once.

Why this, for Rizz

The most heavily run play in the category, and Rizz's own data makes the case: its single best earned mention pulled ~60K views from one committed voice. Orgain runs named ambassadors — @daanawilliamson put 442,733 views and 31,548 total engagements on one ambassador post. Orgain is a looser product match (plant-nutrition shakes), but no gummy brand runs ambassadors here — that is the open lane.

Strategy 03

Creator Collective / Volume Model

Run many micro-creators at once at low per-post cost — manufacture creator posts by volume rather than betting on a few big names.

Why this, for Rizz

This is the play Rizz's exact product twin wins. Grüns — greens in a gummy bear, the same product Rizz sells — runs a creator collective spanning 12K to 1.7M followers for 929,429 views. For a 412-follower brand with zero creator posts, volume is the cheapest, most scalable way to manufacture proof.

Strategy 04

Always-On Creator Retainers

Keep a small standing roster of creators on retainer so there is always fresh creator content flowing, instead of campaign bursts.

Why this, for Rizz

Goli is the only brand in the category running an always-on retainer signal — a clear open lane. Its retained micro-creators (3–12K followers) post on a steady drumbeat. The numbers per post are modest; the value is the always-on flow, not any single hit.

What Rizz should do

Put 3–5 small, on-brand creators on a light monthly retainer for a guaranteed number of posts. At this stage prioritise reliability and fit over reach — a steady trickle of authentic posts builds the creator footprint Rizz currently has none of.

The throughline is simple: every play above is already won by a brand built like Rizz, and Rizz runs none of them. Start with the Creator Collective — it is the cheapest, fastest way to manufacture the creator posts Rizz has zero of, and it is the exact play its product twin Grüns uses.
