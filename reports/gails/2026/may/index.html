Across the 90-day brand window, GAIL's published 47 own-feed posts on Instagram. 22 of them are carousels, 20 are videos, and 5 are photos. The average post pulled 623 likes, with the median sitting lower at 354, the kind of distribution where a small number of standout posts carry the engagement and the rest sit in the workmanlike middle. The averages obscure a more useful signal: the pillar mix.

Recipe and How-To posts averaged 3,700 likes apiece, the highest of any pillar GAIL's runs. Behind-the-Scenes content sat at 1,654. Product Storytelling, the dominant pillar by volume with 18 of 47 posts, averaged 680. Seasonal and Promotional posts ran 434. Lifestyle and Neighbourhood content landed at 421. The pillar that wins per post is the one the brand publishes least, and the pillar that dominates volume is the lowest performer in the set.

Video carries the top of the leaderboard. The five highest-liked own-feed posts include three Reels and two image-led posts, with the carrot cake Reel doing 14× the median post. Across the full 47-post window, Video averaged 864 likes per post and Carousel averaged 351, a 2.5× gap on production format alone before any content-pillar adjustment. Low-production-complexity creative (countertop shots, simple b-roll) tends to outperform polished brand campaigns in this window, which mirrors the survivor pattern in Section 04.

The carrot cake Reel pulled 10,084 likes on a feed where the average is 623. That's a 16× outlier from a pillar that has run three times in the window. The instruction the data is giving the content team is direct: more recipe content, on Reel format, at countertop production cost.

The Biggest Miss

Recipe and How-To content averages 5.4× the likes of GAIL's dominant Product Storytelling pillar. The brand makes it 6% of the time. Flip that ratio and the own feed becomes the most-watched bakery channel in the niche before the next 40 openings hit.
