Across the 37 day brand window, Buns From Home published 27 own-feed posts on Instagram. The split is 12 videos, 7 carousels, and 8 photos. Average post pulled 998 likes; the typical (median) post landed at 635. The averages flatten an interesting signal underneath them: the pillars are not performing evenly.

Behind the Scenes averages 1,874 likes per post on 3 posts in window - including the rank-1 Holland Park goodbye / Hampstead opening carousel at 4,847 likes. Promotional sits at 1,026 likes per post on 4 posts. Inspirational, the dominant pillar by volume (14 of 27 posts, 52% share), averages just 1,017 likes - the floor of the engagement table once Entertaining (477) and Relatable (538) are excluded. The pattern is the inverse-correlation: what wins per-post is published least, what dominates volume performs middling.

Format-wise, carousels lead on average likes (1,680) followed by photos (1,101) and videos (532). Production complexity in the top five is uniformly low - phone photography, founder hand in frame, product-on-counter close-up. The lowest-production posts pull the highest engagement. There is no cinematic top-of-stack here; the audience is voting for the founder-in-frame milestone moment.

The biggest miss

Behind-the-Scenes content averages 1.84× the likes of your Inspirational content (1,874 vs 1,017). You make one Behind-the-Scenes post for every 5 Inspirational posts. Flip that ratio and the own-feed becomes the most-watched bakery channel in the niche before the next openings hit.

07 · The opportunity

Six moves that change the game for Buns From Home.

Six plays, ranked by strategic leverage against the current state of the niche and the brand's earned-mention engine. Four are new programmes the niche has tested at scale; two amplify signals Buns From Home is already running, but at rank 8 of 8 in their respective plays.

Opportunity 01

Build a retainer bench from the creators already tagging unpaid

Fifteen unique creators tagged Buns From Home unpaid in the 37-day window - putting the brand in the top 4% of London Cafés & Bakeries on Creator Tagged Posts. Across the same window, no niche brand averages even 1.3 posts per creator - the entire category is broad-earned, not retainer-driven. The first brand to convert organic taggers into a 3-month retainer bench compounds an already-working signal.
