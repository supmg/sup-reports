CAMPAIGN STRATEGY RECOMMENDATIONS

Four creator plays for Rizz Superfood Gummies, each already proven by a brand built like this one.

Rizz posts roughly seven times a week and earned zero Creator Tagged Posts in the window we analysed. The content engine is running; the distribution engine is not. These are the four plays that put other people's audiences in front of the content Rizz already makes, drawn from the competitors most similar to it that win at each one.

Rizz Superfood Gummies
@rizz_nutrition · DTC superfood greens gummies

What we analysed: 128 brands in the DTC supplements & wellness dataset, the top 144 creators across Instagram and TikTok, the four campaign plays Rizz is most under-running, and the competitors most similar to Rizz that win at each, over a 69-day category window (7 March to 15 May 2026).

Rizz makes modern supplement content. It runs UGC-style video, leans on relatable humour, and even tested early UGC ads. What it does not have is anyone other than Rizz amplifying any of it.

In the 69-day window, Rizz earned zero Creator Tagged Posts (Instagram and TikTok posts where a creator tags the brand) and zero surviving paid creative, while the brands it most resembles compound hundreds of creator posts and thousands of live ads. The fastest path forward is not more posting. It is putting creators in front of the content Rizz already knows how to make.

Each of the four plays below leads with the recommendation, then the proof from the closest comparator, then exactly what to brief. Every number traces to a real cell in the dataset; where a figure was not captured, it says so.

Strategy 01

UGC for Brand Feed

Commission creators to shoot video, then run that video as posts on your own grid instead of filling it in-house.

Why this, for Rizz. This is the highest-reach play in the dataset. Six operators run it, aggregating 21,531,729 views, the largest view pool of any strategy here. It closes Rizz's clearest content gap: Rizz fills its own grid in-house with about 70 posts and zero creator posts, and tops out near 7 likes, yet its video already beats its static posts roughly six times over. Putting commissioned creator video into the feed is the obvious next input for content Rizz already makes.

Strategy 02

Long-Term Brand Ambassador

Keep one or a few creators on a standing relationship so they post about you repeatedly over months, not once.

Why this, for Rizz. This is one of the most heavily run plays in the dataset: 11 operators, 4,530,974 aggregate views, the second-highest match count here. Seven competitors run it, and the two multi-play leaders, Waterboy and Orgain, both lead with it. Rizz's own data makes the case: its single strongest earned mention pulled 59,759 views from one committed creator. Converting that kind of one-off into a standing relationship is how a single voice turns into compounding reach.

Strategy 03

Creator Collective / Volume Model

Run many micro-creators at once at low per-post cost, manufacturing creator posts by volume rather than betting on a few big names.

Why this, for Rizz. This is the play Rizz's exact product twin wins. Four operators run it for 132,188 aggregate views, led by Grüns — greens in a gummy bear, the same product Rizz sells. The findings flag it as an open lane: no brand in this dataset concentrates creators into retainers, so the category runs on broad volume. For a 412-follower start-up, a volume model of many micro-creators is the most scalable, lowest-cost-per-post way to manufacture the Creator Tagged Posts Rizz has zero of.

What Rizz should do
  • Stand up a small bench: retain 3–5 creators who post on a standing schedule, so there is always fresh Rizz content in market. Goli's edge here is repetition from the same faces, not breadth.
  • Profile to brief: mid-and-micro creators with a clear identity hook (a vegan creator like @veganzinga, a relatable-mum voice), whose niche makes the gummy a natural fit.

  • Copy angle: short, punchy, energy-forward lines ("RUN don't walk", "Fuel your potential") with consistent #goli-style brand tags so the bench reads as one ongoing voice.
  • Cadence: a guaranteed monthly minimum per retained creator. The point of a retainer is a steady baseline of creator posts, which is exactly the zero Rizz is starting from.

One throughline, one first move.

All four plays point the same direction: Rizz already makes the content the category rewards, and the brands most like it — Grüns the literal twin, Goli and Olly the gummy twins, Jocko Greens the greens twin — win by putting that content in other people's hands. The gap is not creative. It is distribution. Rizz earned zero Creator Tagged Posts in the window while these comparators compound thousands of creator posts and millions of views on plays Rizz runs on none of.

The fastest first move is the Creator Collective / Volume Model: a batch of 10–20 micro-creators shooting the day-in-my-life gummy content Grüns has already proven at rank #1. It is the lowest cost per post, the most forgiving of a small follower base, and the quickest way to turn a zero into a steady stream of creator posts — which the always-on bench and the ambassador layer then compound.
