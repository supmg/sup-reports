Twenty-one creators tagged PRÉS, and fifteen of the twenty-two posts carry a #partnership disclosure sourced through the Hummingbirds local-creator network, clustered into five metro pods, Atlanta, Houston, Kansas City, Oklahoma City and Orlando, each tied to a nearby Sprouts. This is a deliberate gifting and seeding programme stacked on top of geo-clustered micro-creator activations, and for a brand this size it is sharp work.

Because the calibration here is strong, twenty-one unique creators tagging in the window, the right way to read the paid versus organic split is amplification, not absence. The interesting question this raises is why none of that content is being pushed any further than a single organic view.

Start with scale. Across this niche there are 3,798 active ad creatives from 51 advertisers, and only about 11% of all creatives survive past 60 days, in line with the wider industry. The question is what makes that 11%. Among the longest-running creatives, creator-shot UGC is the single most over-indexed style, 41% of the 60-plus-day winners against 36% of the full top set. The ads that last in this category are filmed by people, not studios.

The durability is real. Bulk keeps a lifestyle creative running 622 days and Humantra a product carousel for 616, but the creator-shot survivors are the instructive ones: MaxiNutrition has a single UGC UGC for paid ads testimonial live for 500 days, Humantra a 367-day UGC piece, and RYZE three cinematic mascot films past 320 days each. This is compounding creative, not a one-off.

Across the 181-day brand window PRÉS published just 14 posts on its own channels, 9 on Instagram and 5 on TikTok, about one every fortnight. The average own post draws 36 likes and the typical one 28, numbers that read as an early account still finding reach, and the low cadence is the first thing the data flags. The brand's creators are out-posting and out-performing the brand's own feed several times over.

By format the signal is sharp. The brand's three carousels average 69 likes, its two photos 32, and its nine videos just 26, so the most-published format on the feed is also the weakest, while the format that works best is barely used. By pillar the feed leans hard on Promotional, 7 of 14 posts, and those promotional posts do hold up at 46 average likes, but the lighter Relatable and Meme content (35 likes) and the calmer Inspirational routine posts (20 likes) round out a feed that is doing too little, too rarely.

The Biggest Miss
