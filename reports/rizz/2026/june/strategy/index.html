Rizz Superfood Gummies — Campaign Strategy Recommendations

Four creator plays Rizz should run — each one a similar gummy/greens brand demonstrably runs: UGC for Brand Feed, Gifting & Seeding, Creator Collective, and Always-On Retainers.

Strategy 01

UGC for Brand Feed

Pay creators to shoot video, then run that footage as posts on your own grid — instead of filling the feed in-house.

Why this, for Rizz

Olly and Lemme fill their own grids with creator-shot UGC rather than polished pack-shots. The Olly own-feed UGC posts shown pulled 5.4M views; Lemme's pulled 1.3M — these run on the brand account, not the creators'. Rizz fills its grid in-house and tops out near three likes a post: the creator-style instinct is right, the creator input is missing.

Strategy 02

High-Value Gifting & Seeding

Send product to a steady stream of creators and let the ones who genuinely like it post — paid in product, not fees.

Why this, for Rizz

Folly — a fellow gummy brand (hair-growth gummies) — is the category's #1 gifting operator: seeded creators put up 33 tagged posts (“Gifted by @follynutrition”, “30 days with the hair gummies”). Rizz already has a dormant earned-mention engine — its best unpaid mention pulled ~60K views — so a structured gifting motion is the cheapest way to restart it.

What Rizz should do

Strategy 03

Creator Collective / Volume Model

Run many micro-creators at once at low per-post cost — manufacture creator posts by volume rather than betting on a few big names.

Why this, for Rizz

This is the play Rizz's exact product twin wins. Grüns — greens in a gummy bear, the same product Rizz sells — runs a 100-creator collective, 76 of them under 50k followers (44 under 10k): a high-volume crowd of micro-creators, not a few big names. The creators shown below are that micro cohort. For a 412-follower brand with zero creator posts, that volume is the cheapest, most scalable way to manufacture proof.

Strategy 04

Always-On Creator Retainers

Keep a small standing roster of creators on retainer so there is always fresh creator content flowing, instead of campaign bursts.

Why this, for Rizz

This is an open lane: effectively no brand in the category runs an always-on retainer. Goli is the only one with any repeat roster at all — and even that is thin: a handful of small creators (1.5K–12K) posting 3–5× each (shown below), not a real standing bench. For Rizz the absence IS the opportunity — a steady retained bench would be the category's first.

What Rizz should do

Put 3–5 small, on-brand creators on a light monthly retainer for a guaranteed number of posts. At this stage prioritise reliability and fit over reach — a steady trickle of authentic posts builds the creator footprint Rizz currently has none of.

The throughline is simple: every play above is already won by a brand built like Rizz, and Rizz runs none of them. Start with the Creator Collective — it is the cheapest, fastest way to manufacture the creator posts Rizz has zero of, and it is the exact play its product twin Grüns uses. Sup can source, brief, and run the creators end-to-end.
