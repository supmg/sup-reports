meCore is one of 92 brands in the global Fashion & Creator Apps niche. Across the 84-day niche window (2026-02-20 to 2026-05-15), the brand sits at rank 53 of 92 on Instagram followers (266 followers, bottom half of the niche) and rank 29 of 92 on Creator Tagged Posts (one tagged post in the window). Direct fashion-styling-app peers (Doji, Indyx, Dressly, Whering) are all running visible creator pipelines while meCore is in waitlist mode.

The Benchmark Verdict

meCore is a pre-launch brand sitting below most of the niche on every external signal. The numbers are not a strategic gap to close, they are a starting line. The interesting question is which competitor pattern meCore should adopt first, and the data already points to one.

02 · Influencer Intelligence

One creator did the work of fifteen brand posts.

Across the 84-day brand window (2026-02-22 to 2026-05-14), meCore picked up one Creator Tagged Post (Instagram and TikTok posts where a creator tagged the brand inside the window). One creator, Anna Figueroa at 15,840 Instagram followers, posted a UGC-style brand story positioned around her habit of photographing random walls. The video pulled 1,053 likes and 25,304 views, a 6.83% engagement rate, with the meCore app appearing as the unlock that translates the wall textures into outfits.

That single post is the highest-performing piece of content in meCore's window across both platforms. Reposted to TikTok it pulled another 33,900 plays. Average likes on the brand's own polished and animated feed posts sit at 46. The one creator-led narrative outperforms the dominant Aesthetic-Montage pillar 16 to 1.

The pattern in the niche is harder to ignore. Doji, at 15,638 Instagram followers, has 24 unique creators tagging it unpaid in the same window. Indyx at 30,204 followers has 13. Whering at 42,069 has 5. meCore, at 266 followers, has the one. The category around meCore already runs on creator pipelines, just not for meCore.

The Anna activation reads as gifted or partner content (the caption ends with a clean waitlist CTA, no explicit disclosure), placing the brand's organic calibration at sparse, fewer than 3 unique creators tagging unpaid means the earned-mention engine is not yet running. The good news: the prototype already works. The hook score on Anna's video is 8 of 10, the audience response is real, and the format is repeatable.

04 · Creator Content in Paid

Adobe Lightroom's longest-running active ad in this niche reached 613 days, with three of the top ten longest-lifetime creatives belonging to Captions and another three to Adobe Lightroom. meCore has zero active Meta ads across a 20-country sweep on 2026-05-19.

The pattern across the three: between five and twelve unique creator-shot creatives in flight at any given time, refreshed roughly every 30 to 60 days. The cycle is what makes the survivors stick.

Across the 84-day brand window, meCore published 39 own-feed posts: 21 on Instagram and 18 on TikTok. The pattern is consistent with the niche's paid-ads winners: UGC outperforms polished brand and animation across both organic and paid in this category.
